Academy Age Categories & Squads

Explore our competitive youth structures ranging from grassroots to elite levels.

Under 11 (U11) - Grassroots Development

Focuses on ball mastery, coordination, and fundamental motor skills in a fun environment.

Under 13 (U13) - Junior Development

Introduction to tactical positioning, small-sided game intelligence, and teamwork.

Under 15 (U15) - Intermediate Competitive

Advanced technical drills, competitive league fixtures, and physical conditioning.

Under 17 (U17) - Elite Youth

High-intensity training, tactical match analysis, and scouting exposure.

Under 19 (U19) - Senior Academy / Pre-Pro

Bridging youth football to professional leagues with rigorous tactical demands and high-level competitive exposure.
